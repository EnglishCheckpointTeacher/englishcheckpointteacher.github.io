Grade 8 English Checkpoint

At this level you're expected to analyse how writers create effects and to write with control and style. Build those skills here.

The exam

Paper 1: Non-fiction

  • Read one or two non-fiction texts, such as articles, speeches or autobiography
  • Explain the writer's viewpoint, language and structure, and compare texts
  • Write an argument, a discussion, a speech or a formal letter

Paper 2: Fiction

  • Read an extract from a story or a poem
  • Analyse character, setting, mood and the writer's choices
  • Write a narrative or descriptive piece

Ask your teacher for the exact timings and dates for your school's exam session.

Exam skills

Press 🔊 to hear any tip read aloud.

Point, Evidence, Explain

Make your point, quote a short piece of evidence, then explain the effect of the writer's words on the reader.

Zoom in on words

Pick one powerful word from a quotation and explore what it suggests. Strong verbs and adjectives carry meaning.

Think about structure

Ask why the writer opens, shifts or ends the text the way they do. Look for short sentences, paragraph changes and changes of focus.

Compare clearly

When comparing texts, use linking words such as "similarly", "whereas" and "in contrast", and refer to both texts in every paragraph.

Argue with power

Use rhetorical questions, facts, statistics, direct address and a counter-argument that you then knock down.

Show, don't tell

In narrative writing, show feelings through actions and senses. Vary your sentence lengths and openings to control the pace.
