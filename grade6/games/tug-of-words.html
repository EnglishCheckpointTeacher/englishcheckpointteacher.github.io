Tug of Words

Two players pull at the same time. The rope sits between them.

• Both girls play together — each answers her own questions on her own side, with no timer and no turns.
• Correct answer → your girl digs her heels in, the rope snaps to your side and the other girl is dragged forward.
• Wrong answer → the rope holds and the correct answer is shown.
• Questions run easy to hard across all nine word classes: noun, pronoun, verb, adjective, adverb, preposition, conjunction, interjection, article.
