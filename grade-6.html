Exam skills

Press 🔊 to hear any tip read aloud.

Find it in the text

For "find" and "give" questions, the answer is written in the text. Underline it, then copy only what you need.

Read between the lines

For "why" and "how do you know" questions, give your idea and back it up with a word or phrase from the text.

Check the marks

A question worth two marks needs two points, or one point and a piece of evidence.

Plan before you write

Spend five minutes planning. Decide on your purpose, your audience and three or four paragraph topics.

Know your text type

A report uses facts and subheadings. A persuasive letter uses opinions and reasons. Instructions use numbered steps and imperative verbs.

Check your punctuation

Leave time at the end to check capital letters, full stops, commas in lists, apostrophes and speech marks.

Practice quiz

Ten quick questions on grammar, punctuation and reading. The questions come in a new order each time.
